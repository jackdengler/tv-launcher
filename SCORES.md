# Quiet Scores: just the fantasy overlay

Quiet Scores puts a live ESPN fantasy scorebug over whatever is playing on a Google TV, without
changing your home screen. It's a separate app from the Quiet launcher.

Download link (always the newest build):

```
https://github.com/jackdengler/tv-launcher/releases/latest/download/quiet-scores.apk
```

## Install (TV remote plus a phone, no computer)

1. **Install Downloader** from the Google Play Store on the TV.
2. **Turn on developer options:** go to **Settings → System → About**, scroll to **Android TV
   OS build**, and click it 7 times.
3. **Let Downloader install apps:** go to **Settings → Apps → Security & restrictions →
   Unknown sources** and turn on Downloader. On some TVs this is under **Settings → Privacy →
   Security & restrictions**. Or just continue: the TV will offer a **Settings** button the
   first time you install.
4. Open **Downloader**, enter the link above, then choose **Go**, then **Install**.

Tip: to type on the TV from your phone, use the **Google TV** app's remote and tap its
keyboard icon.

## Set up

1. Open **Quiet Scores** from the Apps row. It starts on **Turn on the fantasy overlay**:
   choose **Open Accessibility settings**, open **Quiet Scores overlay**, switch it on and
   confirm.
   - If the switch snaps back off, go to **Settings → Apps → See all apps → Quiet Scores** and
     choose **Allow restricted settings**, then try again. If that option isn't there, use
     **Reinstall Quiet** in the app and try again.
2. Add your leagues, whichever way is easiest:
   - **Sign in to ESPN** (log in once on the TV). Quiet Scores finds your teams and fills in
     League 1 and League 2. Use **Find my leagues** to choose different ones.
   - **Set up from your phone:** scan the code on the TV with the phone camera (same Wi-Fi),
     paste your ESPN team page link, and tap **Save to TV**.
3. Pick a **Look** (Scorebug, Card, or Full rosters), **Corner**, **Size**, **Opacity**, and
   **Two leagues** (stacked, or taking turns). Turn **Play pop-ups** on or off, and set the
   **Play delay** so pop-ups match your stream.

**Quick show/hide:** the **Scores on/off** tile in the Apps row flips the overlay with one click.
Add it to your favourites for easy reach.

The scorebug shows during NFL game windows. Set **When to show** to **Always** to test it right
away. **Full scores** shows both leagues with every starter.

## Updating

Just open Quiet Scores. When a new build exists, it offers the update right away; confirm with
**Update**.
